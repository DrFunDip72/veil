# Path to Profitability — Veil

Built for your actual constraints: **minimal capital, limited weekly hours, and a hard seasonal deadline.** This is a realistic timeline, not a best-case one — and it deliberately ends in a decision rather than an income.

## Total cash outlay to get operational

| Item | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Domain (1 yr) | $15 |
| Hosting (Vercel free tier) | $0 |
| Image storage (free tier at this scale) | $0 |
| Utah LLC registration | $54 |
| Attorney review of the image licence | $300–800 |
| **Total to become operational** | **$369–869** |

Without the legal review it is **$69** — but do not skip it. Veil displays copyrighted work with attribution deliberately withheld, which is exactly the thing a boilerplate clause will not cover.

## Time budget

| Activity | Frequency | Time per touch |
|---|---|---|
| Photographer outreach + onboarding | 4–5 per week, weeks 1–8 | 45 min each |
| Showing it to real brides | 5 in week 1, then ad hoc | 20 min each |
| Weekly "here are your numbers" email to the roster | Weekly | 30 min |
| Updating `tracker.md` | Weekly | 15 min |
| Bride-side distribution (posts, group shares) | Weekly, from week 8 | 1 hr |

**Weeks 1–8: roughly 6–8 hours a week.** That is the heavy phase and it is almost entirely outreach.
**Weeks 9–16: roughly 2 hours a week.** Reporting and logging.

## Milestones

- **Week 1:** Five real brides have used it and answered the three questions. The image licence is with an attorney. You know whether brides would message through the app or leave for Instagram.
- **Week 2–4:** 15 photographers onboarded with real photos and real prices. You know the objection rate on published pricing.
- **Week 8:** 30 photographers live. Launch to brides into the November engagement season.
- **Week 12:** First measurable lead flow. First photographer says, unprompted, that she got a booking from it — or does not.
- **Week 16:** **The decision.** Leads per photographer per month is a real number.
- **Week 18+ (only if ≥2 leads):** Convert the roster to paid. **First dollar.**

**Realistic time to first dollar: ~18 weeks — and only if the evidence supports it.**

That is deliberately slow. Charging in week 4 would produce a handful of sympathy subscriptions from photographers who like you, which would teach you nothing and churn by week 12. The free season is not generosity, it is the experiment.

## Building toward a repeatable pace

*Conditional on clearing the week-16 bar. All figures assume $39/month at 95% margin.*

| Pace | Paying photographers | Annual run-rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Utah County only | 25 | $11,700 |
| Wasatch Front | 55 | $25,740 |
| Statewide | 150 | $70,200 |

These are gross run-rates, not profit, and every one of them assumes churn stays low — which happens only if lead flow stays real. A roster that is not getting leads does not hold at any of these numbers.

## When to reassess

Specific, falsifiable, with thresholds:

- **If fewer than 3 of the first 5 brides say they would message through the app** rather than leaving for Instagram — the booking-funnel premise is wrong. Reconsider Veil as a *shortlisting* tool that hands off to Instagram, which is a different and much smaller business. Do this in week 1, before recruiting anyone.
- **If fewer than 15 of 50 photographers contacted will publish real prices** — the compare screen, which is the best feature, cannot exist. Reconsider the model as style-match-only with "request a quote."
- **If leads per photographer per month is under 0.5 at week 16** — stop. It is a distribution problem you cannot out-build, and you will have spent one season and under $900 finding out.
- **If 30 photographers cannot be recruited by week 8** — you have missed the November window. Do not launch into an empty app; wait for May 2027 and use the gap to build automatic style scoring, which is the only durable asset here.
- **If free→paid conversion is under 25% at week 18** — the fee is wrong or the leads are not valued. Try $19/month before concluding the model is dead.

Track all of it in `tracker.md`. The whole point of the free season is to replace every estimate in `BUSINESS-MODEL.md` with a measurement.
