# Execution Plan — Veil

*The sequence from working prototype to a measured answer. The goal of every step below is evidence, not revenue — see `BUSINESS-MODEL.md` for why.*

---

## Step 1 — Show it to five engaged women before you touch the code again (day 1–3)

You are in Utah County during engagement season. Find five women who are engaged or married within the last year, hand them your phone with Veil installed, and say nothing. Watch where they stop.

Three questions, asked identically every time:
1. After the taste test: *"Does that describe what you want?"*
2. After matches: *"Would you message her, or would you go look at her Instagram first?"*
3. *"What would you have done instead of this?"*

**Question 2 is the one that matters.** If every single one says "I'd check Instagram first," Veil is a discovery toy, not a booking funnel — and the business model in the next file is wrong. Better to learn that in three days than in three months.

> Do not pitch. Do not explain the blind mechanic. If it needs explaining, that is the finding.

## Step 2 — Get the image licence drafted (days 1–10, in parallel with everything)

Before a single real photo is uploaded. The licence must grant display rights, display **without visible attribution during the blind phase**, and the right to derive and store style metadata.

Budget $300–800 for an attorney to read it. This is the one step where cheaping out costs real money later. Do it in parallel — it does not block Steps 1 or 3.

Also: register the Utah LLC, and ask the Utah State Tax Commission how SaaS subscriptions are treated for sales tax. Do not assume.

## Step 3 — Recruit 30 Utah County photographers, free (weeks 1–8)

The pitch is specific and short:

> "I built an app where brides swipe wedding photos blind — no names, no prices — and get matched to photographers whose style they actually picked. It's free for you this season. I need 5 engagement, 5 bridal and 5 wedding photos, and your real prices. Here's what your profile would look like." *(send a screenshot)*

Where to find them: Instagram location tags on Utah County venues (Wadley Farms, Sleepy Ridge, Oak Hills), the Provo City Center Temple tag, UVU/BYU photography programs, and existing Utah wedding vendor directories.

Expect the top two objections to be **publishing real prices** and **showing work without a credit**. Have an answer ready for both before the first DM. Log every single contact in `tracker.md` — the response rate is itself a finding.

**Pace: 4–5 per week. Forty-five minutes each. This is thirty hours of unglamorous outreach and it is the actual job.**

## Step 4 — Launch to brides into the November engagement season (week 8)

Not before 30 photographers are live. An empty app burns your one launch window.

Channels, cheapest first: the five women from Step 1 and their group chats, BYU/UVU student Facebook and Instagram groups, TikTok (the blind taste test is natively shareable — *"I swiped 36 wedding photos blind and it guessed my style"* is the entire video), and the photographers themselves posting that they are on it.

**Instrument from day one:** swipes started, swipes completed, shortlists created, compares opened, messages sent. The funnel drop-off tells you which step is broken.

## Step 5 — The operating loop (weeks 8–20)

Weekly, about two hours:

- Report to every photographer: *"You got N profile views and M messages this week."* This single email is what makes the fee payable later. Send it even when N is 0 — especially then.
- Onboard whoever came in from referrals.
- Update `tracker.md`.

## Step 6 — The decision (week 16)

Open `tracker.md` and read one number: **average leads per photographer per month.**

- **≥ 2** — proceed to Tier 2. Convert the roster to paid, build self-serve uploads, expand to the Wasatch Front.
- **0.5–2** — the mechanic works but volume does not. The problem is bride acquisition, not product. Do not build anything; run one more season of distribution experiments.
- **< 0.5** — stop. Write down why in `tracker.md`, keep the prototype in the portfolio, and move on. You will have spent ~$70 and one season.

---

*Resist adding steps between 3 and 6. Every hour spent on the photographer upload flow, a backend, or auth before week 16 is an hour spent on a business that has not yet been shown to exist.*
